CORRECTIONS

Request a correction

Correction requests cover indexing errors, broken source links, transcription problems and identity mappings. They do not change the official publication.

Submission channel. {% if site.corrections_email %} Email {{ site.corrections_email }}. {% else %} A public submission channel has not yet been enabled for this deployment. The operator will publish a verified contact route before public launch; no address is invented here. {% endif %} {% if site.corrections_note %}{{ site.corrections_note }}{% endif %}

What to include

  • The page URL and the record or person identifier shown on the page.
  • The Gazette number, publication date, PDF page and printed page where available.
  • A clear description of the error and the correction requested.
  • A link to, or copy of, the relevant official source passage.
  • For identity corrections: the specific record references and the name variant at issue. Do not send private personal information.

How requests are handled

  1. The cited source page is checked against the stored extraction and checksum.
  2. If the index is wrong, the record is corrected and the build is regenerated.
  3. If an identity mapping cannot be supported, the mapping is withdrawn from publication and the record remains with its published name.
  4. If the official publication itself is disputed, the requester must pursue that with the official publisher. This site does not alter official records.

No response-time commitment is published. Requests are handled by the site operator; operator identity is not published on this site.

What this process does not do

  • It does not remove accurate records of official publications.
  • It does not provide legal advice or determine legal rights.
  • It does not accept bids, payments or account requests.
  • It does not publish private contact details or unsupported personal claims.
