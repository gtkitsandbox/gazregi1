Why this exists

Public records are not the same as findable records.

  1. 01

    Published once, inside a PDF

    Official notices appear in gazettes, supplements and agency portals. There is no single feed across countries, languages or publishers.

  2. 02

    The reference you would never guess

    Tender numbers, ministry names and legal titles vary by publisher. A keyword that works in one country can return nothing in another.

  3. 03

    A deadline does not wait

    A notice forwarded late is a contract already lost. Publication dates, closing dates and source-reported status stay next to every record.
