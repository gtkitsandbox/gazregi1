Public access

Why this exists

Public records are not the same as findable records. The information exists; the access is what is broken.

  1. 01

    Published once, inside a PDF

    Official notices appear in gazettes, supplements and agency portals. There is no single feed across countries, languages or publishers.

  2. 02

    The reference you would never guess

    Tender numbers, ministry names and legal titles vary by publisher. A keyword that works in one country can return nothing in another.

  3. 03

    A deadline does not wait

    A notice forwarded late is a contract already lost. Publication dates, closing dates and source-reported status stay next to every record.

Provenance

How records are checked

Every result says where it came from, with source links preserved beside the index.

  • ✓

    Verbatim excerpt check

    A structured record is rejected unless its source excerpt appears in the extracted text or retrieved official document.

  • ✓

    Page-level provenance

    Gazette number, publication date, PDF page and source checksum are retained wherever the publisher provides them.

  • ✓

    No invented records

    Empty coverage stays empty. Unverified identities are held back, and no name, deadline or source URL is guessed.
